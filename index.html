jw1

Personal WIKI & Schedule Automation Tool / 개인용 WIKI 및 일정 관리 자동화 도구

Overview

jw1 is a private, single-owner automation utility used strictly by its owner to read and organize personal Markdown notes and manage approved personal calendar blocks. It is not offered as a public or commercial multi-user service.

jw1은 소유자 본인의 개인 Markdown WIKI 문서 정리와 승인된 개인 일정 등록을 보조하기 위한 단독 사용자 전용 개인 자동화 도구이며, 불특정 다수를 대상으로 한 공개 상용 서비스가 아닙니다.

How Google Services Are Used

  • Google Drive: Reads and saves the owner's Markdown notes and inbox drafts inside the owner's designated WIKI folders only upon explicit owner-initiated actions.
  • Google Calendar: Checks free/busy availability and creates personal focus blocks or events only after explicit owner confirmation.

Data Handling & Security

All OAuth credentials and retrieved records remain within the owner's private runtime environment. No Google user data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or external model training.

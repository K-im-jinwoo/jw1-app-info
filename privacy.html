Privacy Policy for jw1

Effective Date: 2026-11-18

1. Scope & Purpose

jw1 is a personal-use automation application operated solely by its owner for personal knowledge management and scheduling. This Privacy Policy explains how Google user data is accessed, used, stored, and protected.

jw1은 소유자 본인의 개인 지식 관리 및 일정 보조 목적으로만 운영되는 개인용 앱입니다.

2. Google User Data Accessed

  • Google Drive: Accesses Markdown files and folders within the owner's personal Google Drive WIKI workspace to read notes, detect updates, and write owner-approved memos.
  • Google Calendar: Reads existing calendar events to avoid scheduling conflicts and creates calendar events explicitly approved by the owner.

3. How Data Is Used

Accessed data is used exclusively to execute owner-requested tasks (searching personal notes, summarizing tasks, saving approved memos, and creating approved calendar events). No data is used for marketing, advertising, profiling, or selling to third parties.

4. Data Sharing, Retention & Revocation

  • No Third-Party Sharing: Data is not shared with or transferred to external third-party organizations.
  • Storage: OAuth credentials and synchronized Markdown copies are stored privately in the owner's access-restricted environment.
  • Revocation & Deletion: The owner may revoke access at any time via Google Account Permissions and remove local credential files directly.

5. Google API Services User Data Policy

jw1's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Contact

For questions regarding this application or privacy practices, please contact the owner via the support email configured on the Google OAuth consent screen or through the associated GitHub repository.
